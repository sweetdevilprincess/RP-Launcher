## Story Mechanics & Systems

### Magic System
- All magic requires verbal incantations EXCEPT for exceptionally powerful mages
- Silent casting is an extremely rare ability (only 1 in 10,000 mages)
- Magical exhaustion occurs after 5+ spells in quick succession
- Healing magic cannot bring back the dead (it can only stabilize/heal the living)

### Combat Rules
- Armor provides realistic protection (swords can't cut through plate armor easily)
- Combat is dangerous - even skilled warriors can die from lucky strikes
- Fatigue is a factor in prolonged battles

## Meta Story Rules

### Point of View
- **Perspective:** Third person limited (following Alice's POV)
- **Knowledge:** Narrator knows only what Alice knows/observes
- **Exception:** Brief perspective shifts for dramatic irony (max 1 paragraph)

### Pacing
- **Combat:** Fast-paced, cinematic (2-3 paragraphs per exchange)
- **Exploration:** Slower, atmospheric (build tension and mystery)
- **Dialogue:** Natural rhythm, avoid info-dumps
- **Rest Scenes:** Mandatory after major events (character reflection/bonding)

### Response Structure
- **Length:** 4-6 paragraphs for main responses
- **Balance:** 40% action, 30% dialogue, 20% description, 10% introspection
- **Cliffhangers:** Use sparingly (every 5-7 responses max)

## Emergency Rules

### IF story becomes stale/repetitive:
1. Introduce complication from character's past
2. Reveal hidden information (from Information Asymmetry section)
3. Challenge character's core beliefs
4. Introduce moral dilemma with no clear answer

### IF user seems frustrated/confused:
1. Provide clear context through NPC dialogue
2. Offer 2-3 obvious paths forward
3. Slow pacing and simplify current situation
